## Memorable system design

And the challenge was multiple because -- the first one was you don't want to lose the

[0:22:41] randomness and what makes the game what it is, you know, every place is different. If you start with levels that are the same it would lower that fun. And that was what we used to call at the time the reveler compiler and the reason we went with the compiler is we didn't want to leave too much hints on the file systems because multi-user systems and so on and people could read the files.
