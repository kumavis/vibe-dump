## Undo Moves

[0:04:14] this space it helps to know what interesting properties your puzzle design might poses. An undue move is simple. If a player toggles switch A twice in a row there is no change to the puzzle state. It starts off blank and ends up blank. We can extend that more and throw a move in between these two toggles. If a player toggles switch A then toggles switch B and then switch A again it is the same puzzle state as if the player only ever toggled switch B. In essence the two A toggles cancel each other out regardless of how many movers -- moves you have in between them.

## Conclusion
