## Randomizers as Software

[0:12:20] Next we have randomizer as a software. Straight up, these are automated procedural ROM hacking programs. They are usually external pieces of software from the actual games that go in and transform the underlying actual game that you’re actually playing into a new thing that you’re playing. ## Randomizers as Communities

[0:12:55] As we all know, no system can exist in a vacuum. We require people to go in and play and interact with our software for it to actually have any existence or meaning. Communities build around every single one of these randomizers I have encountered, has a community where people organize to play the game, race each other, teach new people, host events, get the randomizer promoted and get races of it featured in bigger events. At the end of the day, software is made out of people and building strong and inclusive communities around your software is very, very important.
