## Understanding ear fatigue

[0:02:20] What is ear fatigue? It is some someone has heard something so often or so much or at such a volume even that different frequencies start to blur into each other and it makes it hard to distinguish different things. If you have written music and been playing a section over and over and trying to mix things or figure out one specific sound you might get ear fatigue because you have listened to that same portion and you can't distinguish that snare from that one and it is tiring. A roguelike is a game someone will play over and over, ear fatigue is libel to occur to them and you don't really want that to happen. You would like to keep your player engaged in your art as much as you can. Strategies I employ is things like unique segmentation. They might sound obvious when you talk out loud but in practice it makes a difference.

[0:03:22] I am talking about having different chunks of the song so there is an identifiable progress in the loop of music you have created. Some people might look at that as the intro, refrain, chorus, whatever the music theory words are. Some people might just look at that as like actually swapping song halfway through to keep things varied depending if you have a radio system in the game. In essence, the reason you changing things up is to make sure that the player upon hearing this loop is feeling like there is progress being made rather than having it meld into the background.
