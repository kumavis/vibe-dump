## Permadeath

[0:12:18] design elements are to me a package based around permadeath permadeath means playing the start repeatedly and procedural generation is needed to keep that from being repetitive permadeath also means difficulty can't be smoothed out by players replaying hard parts more and procedural generation tends to lead to uneven difficulty because there's less thought and testing going into any particular level consumable resources smooth out that difficulty and can add more tactical depth then turn based games give players time to consider using a variety of consumable items and they smooth out difficulty a little bit because players can play hard parts more slowly so why is permadeath worth designing around that's because it forces players to consider probabilities imagine poker with saves and reloads that would be pretty boring wouldn't it

## Randomness

[0:13:20] when you're heading into the unknown in a randomly generated world your game state is this huge universe of possibility which is much bigger than any particular result permadeath and randomness forces players to engage with that and effectively makes games bigger but remember it is possible to take things too far and to dilute possibilities until they aren't even worth considering now let's talk about dcss specifically
