## Structure versus surface

[0:10:39] I call this next set of lenses the aesthetic locus because it is about where the center of attention is. Are we focused on the artifacts or the generator? Or are we concerned with how does this interact with other generated artifacts or what does this particular artifact do? There is a natural tendency for the player's attention to shift away from the surface and toward the structure as you become more familiar with the generator, but on the other hand, I think in roguelikes, the way that individual details matter a lot tends to keep the player focused on the surface even as they become more familiar with the generator.

[0:11:41] It matters a whole lot if that thing you encounter in a dudgeon is a lower case d or a capital D. You want to be able to tell which one is the dragon.
