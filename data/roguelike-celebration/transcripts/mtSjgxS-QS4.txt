## Defining serial art

[0:01:48] But, many of Sol LeWitt's wall drawings are also exercises in serial art, which explores all the possibilities or consequences of an idea. Here's a more direct example in which he takes 28 photos of the same sphere in almost all combinations of lighting from the top or four sides. Here's a book that LeWitt published with a similar idea. All combinations of arcs from four corners, arcs from four sides, straight lines, not straight lines, and broken lines. So, serial art really takes a step further back into abstraction, playing with the idea of eliminating choice or intuition altogether. Serial art starts with a law or set of axioms or a condition, and produces all the figures or artifacts that satisfy that premise. And typically, these are these enumerated combinations are presented together as a single finished work.
