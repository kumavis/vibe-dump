## Understanding the paradigm

[0:11:25] technical details, goals, and rewards. So, this slide represents the full space of everything that the developers I talked to wanted to talk to me about quests. What I was able to do was take all of that information that they told me and kind of like organize it into this new paradigm, the definition context purpose paradigm. This paradigm starts with the definition which defines the state space of all possible quests in games. Then there's the context. So, that's going to be the set of creative or production constraints the developers actually place on the quest. And the last part is the purpose, which is the reason or reasons that the quest is in the game. As you can kind of see from the picture, the context and the purpose act as a funnel where they take the state space of all possible quests and they narrow it into the set of quests that are actually usable for a particular game. Though not shown in the picture for clarity, a game can have multiple contexts and multiple purposes, and they can overlap as needed in order to more fully explain the design.
