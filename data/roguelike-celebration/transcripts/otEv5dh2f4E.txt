## Causal graphs explained

[0:11:18] This is a picture of a causal graph. Simple one. A causal graph is paid -- made up of things and the arrows between them that represent cause and effect relationships. This causal graph says there is a cause and effect relationship between forest fires and ice cream sales. This doesn't tell you the specific direction of the cause so maybe forest fires make ice cream sales go up, maybe forest fires make ice cream sales go down, but in this, there is some relationship. The way these are typically used is you would take a causal graph and also collect some data and you would pix them together and sprinkle in some math and that would get you a model that you can use to make predictions.

[0:12:21] So the model that you would get from this graph would of course say that forest fires increase ice cream sales so if there are less forest fires than ice cream sales would necessarily go down.
