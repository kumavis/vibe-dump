## Technical constraints

[0:10:26] It was important to display the weapon the character is displaying. It turned out we had only 16 slots available per race to show character tiles. Finally this idea of indexed color. Sil Q crashes in Linux unless the tileset is at 256 color bitmap. This is one of those situations where adding a restriction really improved the tileset.

[0:11:27] I was using way more colors than this forced me to create a custom color table, to be careful about which colors I used, to share colors across tiles, and to be aware of basically every pixel value because there are many, many cases where colors looked the same even side by side but were off by just a few numbers. I think the tileset ended up being more coherent and consistent because of this restriction.
