## 0. Conventions and part names

- **Axes:** front (+Z, azimuth 0) = the nose ridge of mask face A (front.jpg). Face B (back.jpg) at 180°.
  Handles at ±90° (side.jpg and side-b.jpg look at them end-on).
- **Décor is TWO-fold per face**, mirror-symmetric about the nose ridge: one 180° panel per register, the
  same on both faces (back.jpg repeats front.jpg element for element; only the wear differs). The handle
  tabs sit on a second, smaller mask centred at ±90° (side.jpg) — so the mould was very probably four
  parts with seams at 0/90/180/270, the 0/180 seams hidden by the nose ridges and the 90/270 ones by the
  handles [unc].
- **Part names:** `body` (the lathe incl. relief, eye bosses, the low nose ridges and foot ridges),
  `handles` (2 cast loop handles with their heads), `heads` (2 frontal heads in the neck frieze).
- **Registers** (profile.json): `base` 0–20.5, `foot` 21.6–33.4, `mask` 36.0–87.6, `frieze` 87.6–101.8,
  `blades` 101.8–111.4, `lip` 111.4–117.

## b. Form

### b.1 Method and camera
Silhouettes and register borders were read on gridded crops of front.jpg (DP218261), side.jpg and
back.jpg, and on the 4000-px originals. front.jpg: horizontal scale from the rim, 5.43 px/mm (1800-px
copy). The camera is **≈ 3–4° above the rim**, not the "high" view the scout notes describe: the rim
ellipse is b/a ≈ 0.054–0.064 and the foot ellipse matches it (front bottom 1123 px vs sides ≈ 1106).
**Photograph vs published size:** both full views read ≈ 3.5 % (front) to 7 % (side.jpg, higher camera,
perspective) TALLER than 117 relative to the widths; not from elevation (which shortens heights). Heights
are scaled to the published H; the residual is noted, not modelled. The camera model fitted for the flat
faces (KIT.md § Flat faces): orthographic down a normal tilted 4° above horizontal, rms 0.86 mm over 9
landmarks.

## c. Décor reading

- **Blades:** 14 per face (pitch 17.15 developed at r 76.4 [meas]), one on each centre line, bases
  touching; each outlined, with two round spirals over the base and one above; the inverted triangles
  between are plain (and read dark in the photograph).
- **Neck frieze:** per half, out from the head: a vertical hook column, a leiwen window, a U curl, a
  small round eye with a body band (a dragon facing the head), then alternating leiwen windows and
  squared-spiral body units to the handle. On leiwen.
- **Mask (face A, right half, x = developed mm from the ridge):** nose-side column of two C curls
  (x 3–10); framed leiwen window (11–25); the brow block with U ticks, two curls and hooks, leiwen window
  W2 at its right (25–50); the horn block — a G groove rolling into a spiral, a small leiwen window inside
  (50–71); a tall framed block of two big squared spirals (72–81); a plain body band beneath (y 30–34) over
  a strip of tiny spirals. Lower half: the pillow eye (x 20.3, 14 × 11, slit below centre), a slotted
  plate and a curl plate below the nose column, the jaw (a slanted plate with a big nested squared spiral
  and a rolled curl, x 11–34), a row of C scrolls along the eye line (pitch 7.5 from x 37), and a big
  **nested lozenge** filler (research/04 G3) in the lower field. Beyond x ≈ 81 a second, smaller mask
  centred on the handle line, with a small eye low beside the handle (side.jpg).
- **Foot frieze:** short ridges at 0° and 90°; in each quarter two dragons facing those ridges: squared
  head block, small eye, nested body band, tail rolled into a large round spiral.

- **Measured:** silhouette, rim/foot/belly diameters, register heights (±1.5 in y), handle loop, every

  beyond x ≈ 45.
- **Invisible / omitted:** the interior graph, the base lattice, the mask's lower border (under the belly).
