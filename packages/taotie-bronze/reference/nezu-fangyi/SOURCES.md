- I first matched the AIC piece to the owner's photo as the same object, because its lid kui frieze, lid pegs, belly mask and flange rhythm are nearly identical.
- After the owner reported the Nezu location, a feature-by-feature re-check found differences that rule out identity:
  - **Knob:** the Nezu knob's eaves corners end in downward hooked loops and its mini-mask is built of squared spirals. The AIC knob has square, unhooked eaves and a pellet-type mask.
  - **Foot:** the Nezu foot-centre flange ends in a pale rounded tab hanging onto the plinth ledge. The AIC foot-centre flange stops flush.
- The AIC piece is therefore a **near-twin from the same design family** (very likely the same workshop and generation), not this vessel.
- Both Commons files were downloaded as 1280-px renditions and re-encoded (JPEG q90, metadata stripped). CC BY-SA terms apply if the Harrsch file is redistributed. The app does not ship it.
- Other fangyi inspected and rejected as comparanda of lower relevance: Met 43.24.5a–b (mid-10th c. BC), Freer F1930.54a–b (Ling fangyi 令方彝), Luoyang Museum fangyi (Gary Todd, Commons), Harvard Art Museums fangyi (Daderot, Commons).
