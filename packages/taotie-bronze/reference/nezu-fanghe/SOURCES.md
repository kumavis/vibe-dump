# Sources — nezu-fanghe

**饕餮文方盉 · Three Square He with Taotie Design** — Nezu Museum, Tokyo (根津美術館). Bronze.

## Facts from the museum label (transcribed from the card photograph)

- Title: 饕餮文方盉 / Three Square He with Taotie Design
- Date: Shang dynasty, 13th–12th centuries BC
- Provenance: Said to have been excavated from Houjiazhuang, Anyang city, Henan province (伝河南省安陽殷墟侯家荘出土)
- Designation: Important Cultural Property (重要文化財)
- Number on the card (lower right): 90064, 90065 (two cards photographed; the group is three vessels)
- Exhibit number in the gallery: 1
- Dimensions: not on the label.

## Which vessel each photograph shows (sorted in round 7)

The museum shows the three he of the set side by side, and they differ in detail, so every frame was sorted
before anything was measured. Two of the three are photographed; the third appears only in backgrounds.

- **Vessel A** — spout with ONE mushroom-capped peg on its upper side and a ladder flange along the upper
  edge (no wings); handle head a dragon/tiger with open jaws in profile, its horns flat rounded-square plates
  with a NOTCHED (toothed) rim; décor as the flattened faces show.
- **Vessel B** — spout with a pair of broad flat WINGS spreading sideways half-way up and a ladder flange on
  both the inner and outer side; handle head with a hooked, beak-like profile, its horns smooth C-scrolls with a
  spiral inside (no teeth); an extra band of plumed horizontal creatures under the frieze on the spout face.

**Round 8 — a lid conflict.** Read with the handle face as +Z, photos 43 and 44 put the crenellated ridge on the
−X side of the spine, the filling hole beyond the ridge's handle-side end and the spout root at the ridge's other end;
photos 40 and 41 show the SAME layout MIRRORED (ridge, hole and spout on +X). Side photos 39 and 42 show the ridge on
the cushion's top silhouette from both sides (so it is close to the spine) and, from +X, the toothed jaw plate in front
— consistent with 43/44. Either 40/41 show the third vessel of the set (a mirror twin with the same peg spout and
notched horns) or one pair was read wrongly; the model follows 39/42/43/44 (ridge x ≈ −28, hole x ≈ −22, spout
root x ≈ −22), so its lid does not match the `three-quarter` reference (photo 40) on that point.

**THE model is vessel A** (eight frames, including both side views, the handle close-ups and the lid). Vessel B
is used as a labelled comparandum only for what A's frames do not show: A's spout (−Z) face is never seen head-on;
photo 24 (B) shows that face exists with the same lobe masks and frieze heads, and A's back is built on that
pattern WITHOUT B's extra plumed band. Vessel B's photographs are never a view's `ref`.
