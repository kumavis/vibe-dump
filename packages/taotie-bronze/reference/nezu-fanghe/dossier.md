Compact. **M** = measured off the owner's photographs (method given), **I** = inferred, **N** = not visible.
Frame: mm, Y up, feet on y = 0, front (+Z) = the HANDLE face, the spout leans back over −Z.

## What the label states
饕餮文方盉 · Three Square He with Taotie Design · Shang dynasty, 13th–12th centuries BC · said to have been
excavated from Houjiazhuang, Anyang city, Henan · Bronze · Important Cultural Property · Nezu Museum · card
nos. 90064 / 90065 (exhibit 1). No dimensions.

## Photograph vs brief — where the photograph wins
1. **The "sculpted head on the front" IS the handle.** The brief lists a loop handle with an animal head on one
   side AND a separate high-relief head in the crotch on the front. The side views (39, 42) show one sculpture:
   a dragon whose horned head sits at the top of the loop under the eave, whose body is the loop, and whose
   lower body runs down the crease between the two lobes of that face to a small head with a pale stud at the
   top of the V between the legs. So "front" = the handle face, and the views "front" and "handle side" are one.
2. **The crotch is a tall V, not a low arch**: the gap between two lobes rises to ≈ 212 mm (0.49 E), above the
   lower half of the masks (flattened faces).
3. **Three vessels, two photographed.** Photos 13–16 and 24 show vessel B (winged spout, smooth C-scroll horns,
   beaked handle head, an extra plumed band on the spout face); they are not measured.

## Scale (I)
Nothing in frame gives an absolute length: the label cards are photographed separately (card-17, card-46) and the
plinths' size is unknown. The lead's recollection (71–73 cm) was tested for consistency only: with H = 720 mm to
the spout lip, the lobe face is ≈ 210 mm across, the leg ≈ 32–52 mm thick and the eave ≈ 332 mm — the
proportions of a large he, and the leiwen cells come out at ≈ 6–9 mm (1.2–1.3 mm pitch), plausible for a vessel
this size (research/01: pitch ≈ 0.004 × H would give 2.9 mm, which is too coarse for what photo 45 shows). **H
720 mm is an inference, not a measurement**; every number below scales with it.

## Form (M unless marked)
- **Lobes**: four, one per corner, each a pouch whose outer corner is a straight arris under a notched flange and
  whose inner edge sweeps down into the V between the legs; faces bow ≈ 7 mm (I) and meet in a rounded crease
  ≈ 13 mm deep above the V. **Legs**: integral, square-ish section 52 → 32 mm (I from widths in 42), outer faces
  flush with the corner.
- **Frieze** 255–297, **collar** 297–430: concave, flaring to ≈ 304 mm under the eave; notched flanges on its
  corners run out under the eave corners into hooks (41: the hook rises at the corner).
- **Eave**: flat slab 12 mm thick (M on 45), 332 mm square, a row of squared spirals on the fascia (45).
- **Cap**: a cushion, steep at a short skirt, rounding to a broad plateau ≈ 58 mm above the eave (I: the far edge
  rides up in every view). **Filling hole** (real opening): a kidney-shaped oval ≈ 48 × 31 mm on the spine near
  the handle side (15 — vessel B, 41/43/44 — A). **Ridge**: a crenellated ridge of ≈ 4–5 blocks on the spine
  between the hole and the spout root (42, 39, 44), ≈ 17 mm tall.
- **Spout**: hollow tube Ø ≈ 46 mm (M 42), bore ≈ 30 (I), lip at 720 (I via H), a ladder flange along its
  upper side (42, 44), one capped peg on the upper side ≈ 44 % up (39, 42, 44), a dragon in relief on its sides (44).
- **Handle**: loop ≈ 46 mm wide (45), standing ≈ 60–70 mm off the lobe face (39), from under the eave to the
  frieze; dragon head ≈ 66 mm wide with open jaws (39, 42) and two notched rounded-square horn plates ≈ 60 mm
  across (45); lower body down the crease to a small head at ≈ 205 mm with a pale stud (45: a repair pin? N).
- **Frieze heads**: a small sculpted mask at the frieze centre of the three faces without the handle (42; 39).
- **Flanges**: lobe corners ≈ 17 mm proud, T-notched, ≈ 13 mm pitch (45: ≈ 9 teeth over the lobe); frieze
  corners, collar corners, collar centres and the creases of the side and back faces carry square-notched flanges.

## Décor (traced: faces/face-front.jpg from 25, faces/face-side.jpg from 42; relief heights I from shading)
Per half-lobe (canonical x' from the crease to the corner = 105; y world): double G-horn — two concentric bands
≈ 10–12 mm wide (outer top 244, inner top 232) running along the top, down the corner side to ≈ 190, back along
the bottom into a large rolled coil centred ≈ (47, 211) with a deep pocket; a brow bar at ≈ 182 whose corner end
turns down; a C-hook with a pocket at ≈ 165; a domed eye ≈ 19 × 16 at ≈ (80, 151); a round boss ≈ (56, 140).
Bands stand ≈ 5 mm proud with rounded shoulders (I). Ground: squared spirals, ≈ 6–9 mm cells (45, left strip).
Frieze: one fat-bodied kui per half (body ≈ 15 mm), head at the centre line, tail rolled up at the corner.
Collar: N (deep shade in every frame of A): left plain; only its flanges are built. Cap and plateau: rolled C-hooks, oval pillow
plates and a ringed boss (41, 44) — approximate (I), the lid is only seen obliquely. Spout: jointed dragon
bodies on both sides (44) — approximate.
