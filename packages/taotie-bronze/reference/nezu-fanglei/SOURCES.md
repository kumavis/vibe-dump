# Sources — nezu-fanglei

**饕餮文方罍 · Square Lei with Taotie Design** — Nezu Museum, Tokyo (根津美術館). Bronze.

## Facts from the museum label (transcribed from the card photograph)

- Title: 饕餮文方罍 / Square Lei with Taotie Design
- Date: Shang dynasty, 12th–11th centuries BC (中国・殷時代 紀元前12〜前11世紀)
- Material: Bronze (青銅)
- Collection: Nezu Museum (根津美術館)
- Number on the card (lower right): 90081
- Exhibit number in the gallery: 13
- Dimensions: not on the label. The model's H = 503 mm is INFERRED (dossier.md §a.3).

Credit line: photograph by the project owner, Nezu Museum, Tokyo, 2026
