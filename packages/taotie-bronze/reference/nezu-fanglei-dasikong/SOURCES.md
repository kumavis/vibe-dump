# Sources — nezu-fanglei-dasikong

**饕餮文方罍 · Square Lei with Taotie Design** — Nezu Museum, Tokyo (根津美術館). Bronze.

## Facts from the museum label (transcribed from the card photograph)

- Title: 饕餮文方罍 / Square Lei with Taotie Design
- Date: Shang dynasty, 13th–12th centuries BC
- Provenance: Said to have been excavated from the Dasikong site, Anyang city, Henan (伝河南省安陽殷墟大司空村出土)
- Designation: Important Cultural Property (重要文化財)
- Number on the card (lower right): 90079
- Exhibit number in the gallery: 12
- Dimensions: not on the label.
