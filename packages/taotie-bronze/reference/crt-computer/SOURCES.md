# Sources: crt-computer (a desktop computer with a CRT monitor, dressed as a Shang bronze)

A Taotie Kit showpiece, not a museum object. There are **no photographs** in this folder and none were used.

**Form.** A generic desktop personal computer of the late 1980s / early 1990s, of no particular make, drawn from
common knowledge of the type — no manufacturer's model, badge, logo or proportions were copied:

- a horizontal ("desktop") system unit: a wide, low box with a 3.5-inch floppy drive (a slit and an eject button), a
  second 5.25-inch-class bay below it, a power button, two indicator lamps, horizontal vent grooves, four short feet;
- a 14-inch-class CRT monitor standing on it: a boxy housing with a deep bezel round a convex 4:3 screen with rounded
  corners, the housing tapering backward into the tube's truncated pyramid and a short tube block, a swivel foot and
  neck, a row of three knobs under the screen, vent slots on the top and sides;
- a 101-key-style keyboard lying in front: a low wedge, staggered rows (function row in groups of four, number row,
  QWERTY rows with Tab / Caps / Shift / Enter widths, a 7-unit space bar between Ctrl and Alt), a numeric pad with a
  tall + and Enter and a double 0, three lock lamps; its cable runs back to the unit.

**Scale.** Chosen, not measured: system unit 420 × 360 × 112 mm on 18 mm feet; monitor housing 340 × 306 mm at the
front, 312 mm deep, the bezel 344 × 310 with a 262 × 197 mm opening; keyboard 404 × 152 mm, 15 → 31 mm thick,
key pitch 19.05 mm (the standard). Front +Z, the set centred on x = 0, standing on y = 0.

**Décor.** Designed for this piece with the kit's rounded vocabulary (KIT.md § Rounded bands) and companion motifs
(whorls, bosses, scales, lozenge ground), calibrated by eye on the project's Met fangyi and Nezu you reference
photographs; no single bronze is copied. The casting idea (the lead's): the screen is a polished bronze mirror (a real
genre: a plain, gently convex, bright face); the bezel carries a taotie whose open mouth is the screen; kui dragons
meet at the floppy slot; the key-caps are the nipple-boss grid (乳钉) of Shang ding and gui; notched flanges run down
every arris; the cable is a scaled snake biting the keyboard.
