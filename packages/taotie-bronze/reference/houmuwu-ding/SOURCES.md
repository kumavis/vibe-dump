# Sources — houmuwu-ding reference images

All files were downscaled to at most 1800 px on the long side (ImageMagick `-resize '1800x1800>' -quality 86`). EXIF was stripped. The object is in the **National Museum of China, Beijing**. None of these images comes from the museum itself. They are visitors' photographs and one historical rubbing, published on Wikimedia Commons. Licences were checked against the Commons API (`prop=imageinfo&iiprop=extmetadata`) in November 2026.

Text sources for identity and dimensions (not images):

- zh.wikipedia, 后母戊鼎. Gives H 133 cm, mouth 110 × 79 cm, 832.84 kg, legs 46 cm, wall 6 cm, hollow legs. It also gives the décor reading (扉棱上为牛首，下为饕餮; 鼎耳外廓…两虎…人头; 耳侧鱼纹; 足…三道弦纹之上各施以兽面), the casting facts (handles pre-cast and set into the mould; body one pour from 28 clay mould sections) and notes that one handle is a later replica.
- en.wikipedia, Houmuwu ding (context only).
- Camera focal lengths for front/side/three-quarter come from the files' EXIF on Commons (`iiprop=metadata`).

Comparanda considered but not copied: `File:20251026 Hou Mu Wu Ding 01/02.jpg` (CC BY-SA 4.0, Windmemories; the download was rate-limited), `File:63084-Beijing-National-Museum-of-China (28681867806).jpg` (CC BY 2.0), and the 1948 photograph `File:Chiang and HouMuWu Ding.jpg` (PD; it shows the vessel before modern cleaning).
