# Sources

Where the words, the stones and the map's terms come from. The page carries
one line of credit in its stats plate; this is the full account.

## The words and the stones

The word list (`src/data/words.js`) and the root glossary (`src/data/roots.js`)
are generated by `research/roots/build_words.py` from the word-by-word review
in `research/review/`. Each word was read against these sources:

- **Wiktionary**, Hawaiian entries, by Wiktionary contributors, licensed
  under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/);
  read through the [kaikki.org](https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Hawaiian/)
  extraction (Tatu Ylonen, *wiktextract*). Modern spellings, senses, root +
  root analyses and homographs. The card glosses are short paraphrases of
  these senses and are shared under the same licence.
- **Lorrin Andrews, *A Dictionary of the Hawaiian Language*, revised by
  Henry H. Parker** (Honolulu, 1922). Public domain; OCR from the
  [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/ofhawadictionary00andrrich).
  Attests most of the compounds and names their parts, but writes no ʻokina
  or kahakō, so its spellings were never used as they stand.
- **POLLEX-Online**, the Polynesian Lexicon Project: Greenhill, S. J. &
  Clark, R. (2011). POLLEX-Online: The Polynesian Lexicon Project Online.
  *Oceanic Linguistics* 50(2), 551–559. <https://pollex.eva.mpg.de/>
  (© POLLEX-Online, Simon J. Greenhill, Ross Clark & Bruce Biggs). Each
  stone's ancestor (*PPN \*wai*) and its cognates in other Polynesian
  languages. POLLEX states no open licence, so only those short citations
  are carried; its pages are not redistributed. Its Hawaiian forms are
  almost all cited from Pukui & Elbert.
- **Hawaiian Wikipedia**, used only to count how often a spelling appears in
  running text, as one word or two. Nothing from it is shown.

The authority for Hawaiian spelling and sense is Mary Kawena Pukui and
Samuel H. Elbert, ***Hawaiian Dictionary***, revised and enlarged edition
(University of Hawaiʻi Press, 1986). Its online home refuses automated
access, and that was respected, so it was not read for this list. A word
marked with an open circle on its card (`ev: 'pending'` in the data) has not
yet been confirmed against it; the legend calls this *awaiting dictionary
check*. The list has also not yet been read by a fluent speaker, which is why
the piece stays marked as unfinished in the gallery.

## The map and the star compass

- **The star compass** — its 32 houses, their names and order, and the rule
  that a star sets in the house of the same name it rose in — is the
  Hawaiian star compass of **Nainoa Thompson and the Polynesian Voyaging
  Society**, as published in the
  [PVS archive](https://archive.hokulea.com/navigate/stars.html) and on
  [hokulea.com](https://worldwidevoyage.hokulea.com/education-at-sea/polynesian-navigation/the-star-compass/)
  (the archive cites Will Kyselka, *An Ocean in Mind*, pp. 96–97). The house
  names are Nainoa Thompson's, not ancient; the compass on the floor carries
  a small credit line.
- **Every other Hawaiian term on the map** — *ala loa*, *ahu*, *kuapā*,
  *mākāhā*, *ʻauwai*, *loʻi kalo*, *kauhale*, *hālau waʻa*, *wao akua*,
  *Koʻolau*, *Kona*, *mauka*, *makai* and the lava types — was checked against
  the sources listed term by term in
  [`research/TERMS.md`](./research/TERMS.md): the Polynesian Voyaging
  Society, the National Park Service, University of Hawaiʻi material,
  Wiktionary and Andrews–Parker.
- The island itself is made up. It is generated from the seed, carries no
  place names, and its ahupuaʻa are left unnamed.

## The research

Everything behind the list is in [`research/`](./research):
[`ROOTS.md`](./research/ROOTS.md) (whether two-root compounds can carry the
piece, and the sources table), [`review/`](./research/review) (the review
itself, its decisions and its critic),
[`TERMS.md`](./research/TERMS.md) (the map and compass terms),
[`STRUCTURE.md`](./research/STRUCTURE.md) (the structural analysis) and
[`ENGINE.md`](./research/ENGINE.md) (what the engine needs on a small list).
The scripts there rebuild all of it from the sources above.
