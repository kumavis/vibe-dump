You hatch in the cradle, ten kilometres down.

Drag (or move the mouse) to swim. Eat anything smaller than you to grow biomass and evolve — from larva to The Kraken and worse. From your third form on, every body comes with its own power: tap to use it. Every depth has its own residents, and they fight back — ink, venom, voltage, teeth. Outgrow them, and yesterday's terror is tomorrow's snack. The shallows are thick with food, and with boats. Rise far enough and their hulls become food too. And after the boats? Keep eating.
