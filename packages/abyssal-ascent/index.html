ABYSSAL ASCENT

Larva
The Cradle · 10 500 m biomass 0

You hatch in the cradle, ten kilometres down.

Drag (or move the mouse) to swim. Eat anything smaller than you to grow biomass and evolve — from larva to The Kraken and worse. From your third form on, every body comes with its own power: tap to use it. Every depth has its own residents, and they fight back — ink, venom, voltage, teeth. Outgrow them, and yesterday's terror is tomorrow's snack. The shallows are thick with food, and with boats. Rise far enough and their hulls become food too. And after the boats? Keep eating.

You have eaten the moon.

The tides are yours now. The fleets tell no more stories, because there is no one left who doubts them. And far below, past the vent-lit cradle where you hatched, the seafloor has split open. Something down there is still burning. It smells like food.

You have eaten the heart of the world.

The moon, the fleets, the fire under the floor — all of it, eaten. The salt sea holds nothing you have not tasted. But far inland there is water with no salt in it at all: a bottomless sweet lake with its own old things swimming in the dark. New waters. New teeth.
