ABYSSAL ASCENT

Larva
The Cradle · 10 500 m biomass 0

You hatch in the cradle, ten kilometres down.

Drag (or move the mouse) to swim. Eat anything smaller than you to grow biomass and evolve — fourteen forms, from larva to The Kraken and far, far worse. From your third form on, every body comes with its own power: tap to use it. The residents fight back at every depth — and the ocean is only half the climb. Grow wings, shred the storm layer, graze on satellites, and when nothing on Earth is left to fear, look up. The moon is edible. It disagrees.

You have eaten the moon.

The tides are yours now. The fleets tell no more stories, because there is no one left who doubts them. Somewhere below, in a vent-lit cradle, something small watches the new dark sky — and starts to eat.
