Aperiodic Garden

A garden laid out on the hat — the 13-sided einstein monotile that tiles the plane and never repeats. Three hats pinwheel around a snow-capped peak; a river runs off it.

  • Move the mouse to choose where the next hat goes. Only legal spots are offered.
  • Scroll (or Q/E) to cycle the ways it fits there — twelve orientations, mirrored ones included.
  • Click to lay it. A river must meet a river, so a stream can never dead-end into a bank.
  • Ring a meadow, forest, hills, hamlet or river completely and it seals — big points, and more tiles.

The garden behind this card is laying itself. Yours starts from the peak.
